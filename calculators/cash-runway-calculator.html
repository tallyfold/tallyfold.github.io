Cash runway calculator

How long will the money last? Enter what is in the bank, what comes in each month and what goes out. See how many months you have and roughly when the balance reaches zero.

Your runway

7.5 months

How it is worked out

Take what goes out each month and subtract what comes in. That is your monthly shortfall. Divide the money in the bank by the shortfall and you have the number of months it will last. With £6,000 in the bank, £3,200 coming in and £4,000 going out, the shortfall is £800 a month, so the money lasts 6,000 divided by 800, which is 7.5 months.

If more comes in than goes out, there is no shortfall and the balance grows instead.

Where a single figure falls short

This calculator treats every month as the same. Real months are not: a quiet January, an annual insurance payment or a tax bill can take the balance below zero months before the average says it will. A month-by-month forecast shows that.

The 12-Month Cash Flow Forecast lets you enter each month separately, shows the closing balance for every month and marks any month that ends below zero.
