Shift hours and wage cost calculator

Enter when a shift starts and ends, the unpaid break and the hourly rate. See the paid hours and what the shift costs, for one shift, a week and a year.

Paid hours for the shift

8.00 hours

How it is worked out

  1. Shift length is the end time minus the start time. 09:00 to 17:30 is 8 hours 30 minutes.
  2. The unpaid break comes off. 8 hours 30 minutes less 30 minutes is 8 paid hours.
  3. Wage cost is paid hours times the hourly rate. 8 hours at £13.50 is £108.00.

Shifts that run past midnight

If the end time is earlier in the day than the start time, the shift is treated as finishing the next day. 22:00 to 06:00 is 8 hours, not minus 16. A shift that starts and ends at the same time counts as zero hours, so enter a 24-hour shift as two shifts.

What the cost leaves out

The figure is hours times hourly rate and nothing else. It does not include employer National Insurance, pension contributions or holiday pay, which add to what a member of staff really costs you. Whether a break is paid or unpaid depends on the contract: enter 0 if the break is paid.
