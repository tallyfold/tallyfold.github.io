Stage payment calculator

Split the price of a job into a deposit, stage payments and a final payment. Every amount is rounded to the penny and the total always matches the price.

Each stage payment

How it is worked out

  1. The deposit and the final payment are the percentages you enter. 10% of £36,500 is £3,650 each.
  2. What is left is shared equally between the stage payments. £29,200 over 4 stages is £7,300 a stage.
  3. Each amount is rounded to the penny. Any odd pennies go on the last payment, so the payments add up to the price exactly.

Why split a job into stages

On a job that runs for weeks you pay for labour and materials long before the work is finished. Stage payments tied to points the customer can see, such as foundations complete or roof on, keep money coming in as you spend it. A final payment held until completion gives the customer something to hold back until the snagging is done.

Agree the stages and amounts in writing before you start, and put them on the quote.

A note on VAT

The VAT box adds one rate to every stage. Some building work is charged at a different rate or under the reverse charge. This calculator does not decide that for you: check which applies to the job.
