How it works

  1. Choose a tool and pay once.
  2. Download the Excel file straight away.
  3. Type over the example figures with your own.

Free to use

  • Free 3-month cash flow forecast template: an Excel file to download and keep, with no sign-up.
  • Cash runway calculator: how many months your money will last at the rate you are spending it.
  • Markup and margin calculator: the price to charge for a job to hit the profit margin you want.
  • Day rate calculator: the day rate and hourly rate to charge when you work for yourself, allowing for costs, time off and days you cannot bill.
  • Shift hours and wage cost calculator: paid hours and wage cost for a shift, with breaks taken off and shifts past midnight handled.
  • Stage payment calculator: split a job price into a deposit, stage payments and a final payment that add up exactly.
  • Rental yield calculator: the gross and net yield on a rental property, and what is left each month before tax and mortgage.
  • Break-even calculator: how many sales a month you need before the business covers its costs, and how many more for the profit you want.
  • Late payment interest calculator: the statutory interest and the fixed debt recovery sum you can charge another business for a late invoice.
  • Mileage claim calculator: work out a business mileage claim at the 2026/27 HMRC approved rates.
