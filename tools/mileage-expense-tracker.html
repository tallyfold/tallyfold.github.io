Questions

Which rates does it use?
The HMRC approved mileage rates for the 2026/27 tax year: 55p a mile for the first 10,000 business miles in a car or van, then 25p. You can change them in Settings.
Is this tax advice?
No. It is a record-keeping tool. Check your own position with HMRC guidance or an accountant.
What do I need to open it?
Microsoft Excel. The file has also been tested in LibreOffice Calc. It contains no macros.
Are the figures in it real?
No. Each file comes with example figures to show the format. You type over them.
